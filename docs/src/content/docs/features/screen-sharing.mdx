Screen sharing is one of Hopp's core features. You can share an entire display or, on macOS, a single app window. Teammates can then point, draw, or control your computer without asking you to switch tools.

## How to share your screen

1. **Start a call or join a room**
2. **Click the screen share button** in the control panel
3. **Choose what to share** when the picker appears
4. **Click the screen or window** you want to share

## Window sharing

Window sharing is only supported on macOS for now. Click **Share Window** in the top-right of the picker, then hover over a visible window and click it.

To open the picker in Window mode by default, choose **Window** under **Settings > Screen share settings > Default picker mode**.

## Hide apps with App Veil

On macOS, [App Veil](/features/app-veil) hides selected apps while you share an entire display. You can still see and use them normally. Teammates see a dark area labeled **Hidden with App Veil** in place of each hidden window.

Choose which apps to hide in **Settings > App Veil**. During a call, you can reveal individual windows with the eye button in the **Hopp App Veil** panel.
