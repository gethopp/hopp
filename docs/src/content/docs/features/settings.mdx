The Hopp desktop app provides a Settings panel to customize your call, camera, audio, and miscellaneous preferences.

<Image src={settings} class="rounded-md" alt="Hopp settings panel" />

## Call

**Call feedback popup** — Show a feedback popup when a call ends. Default: on.

**Show dock icon when in call** (`show_dock_icon_in_call`) — Keep the dock icon visible during a call. Default: on.

## Camera

**Start camera on call start** — Automatically open the camera when a call starts. Default: off.

**Camera input resolution** (`camera_input_resolution`) — Request 1080p (1920×1080), 1440p (2560×1440), or 4K (3840×2160) from your camera. Default: 1080p. Hopp saves your choice and reopens an active camera when you change it.

The requested resolution is best effort: the camera or its driver may supply the closest supported format, including a different aspect ratio. For example, a camera without a 2560×1440 mode may supply 1920×1440 instead. This setting controls camera capture; it does not change Hopp's outgoing video resolution or frame rate. When using a virtual camera, its source-camera settings are also controlled by the virtual-camera application.

## Audio

**Start microphone on call start** (`start_mic_on_call`) — Automatically unmute the microphone when a call starts. Default: on.

## Screen sharing

**Enable remote control by default** — Allow teammates to control your computer when screen sharing starts. Default: on.

**Resolution** — Set the maximum screen share resolution to 1080p, 1440p, or 4K. Default: 4K.

**Default picker mode** — Choose whether the screen share picker opens in Screen or Window mode. This setting is only available on macOS. Default: Screen.

## App Veil

On macOS, open **App Veil** in the Settings sidebar to choose which apps to hide when sharing an entire display. Search for an app under **Add application**, then use its switch to turn hiding on or off. Notification Center is in the list by default, with its switch off.

See [App Veil](/features/app-veil) for screenshots and how to reveal individual windows during a call.

## Shortcuts

Global keyboard shortcuts for controlling calls from anywhere on your system. All shortcuts are customizable in the Settings window.

| Action              | macOS         | Windows / Linux |
| ------------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| Toggle microphone   | `Cmd+Shift+A` | `Ctrl+Shift+A`  |
| Toggle camera       | `Cmd+Shift+V` | `Ctrl+Shift+V`  |
| Toggle screen share | `Cmd+Shift+S` | `Ctrl+Shift+S`  |
| End call            | `Cmd+Shift+W` | `Alt+Q`         |

## Miscellaneous

**Custom Backend URL** — Override the default backend server. Leave empty to use the default Hopp backend. This is useful for [self-hosted Hopp instances](/open-source/self-hosting).
